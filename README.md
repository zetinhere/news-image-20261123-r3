The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026, drawn as a clock. One
24-hour dial holds the date in its centre; eight stories from the day
sit on the dial like hours, each with its own drawn pictogram, and
their headlines run in two columns beside it, joined to the dial by
leader lines. The stories: COP31 collapses in Antalya with no deal on
fossil fuels; Russian missiles enter Poland and NATO is called to
emergency talks; Ukraine sinks the Russian corvette Mercury off
Benghazi; Iran hits Bahrain and tankers are struck in the Strait of
Hormuz; an aid truck is hit in Gaza as Oxfam warns 99% of it is
contaminated; Democrats take 258 House seats; South Korea's education
minister quits over AI exam cheating; and Denmark foils an alleged
Russian plot to kill a rapper at a Copenhagen concert.

## Run it

```sh
python3 draw.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`draw.py` draws `entry.png` from scratch with PIL (no network, no
downloaded images, no randomness — the same command always writes the
same bytes); `main.ts` copies it to the output directory, world-readable.
